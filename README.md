# Parent-Care-Reminder
An AI-powered app that reminds busy people to call and spend time with their parents. Because parents deserve our love every day! ❤️## 💝 Why This Project?
In today's busy world people forget
to call their parents because of 
jobs and other works. This app 
reminds you to never forget them!

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Python
- Artificial Intelligence
- Natural Language Processing

## 🔔 Features
- Daily call reminders for parents
- Send love messages to parents
- Track last time you called them
- Birthday and special day alerts
- Appreciation quotes for parents

## 🎯 Goals
- Remind busy people about parents
- Strengthen family relationships
- Spread love and care for elders

## 💌 Message
"No matter how busy life gets,
never forget the people who gave
you everything. Call your parents
today!" ❤️
